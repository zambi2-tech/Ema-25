How support is detected

On each timeframe, the app looks at the last 40 closed candles. The EMA 25 counts as support when all of these are true:

  • The EMA 25 is sloping up.
  • Since price last closed below the EMA, it has dipped to it and closed back above it at least twice. A dip counts when a candle's low comes within about a quarter of a normal candle's range of the EMA.
  • No candle has closed below the EMA since then.

A gold line under a cell means support is holding, and the number shows how many bounces it has had. A gold box means price is sitting on that support right now. A red dashed line means price has dropped below a support that was holding; the break is confirmed if the candle closes there.

These are rule-based signals from past candles, not predictions.
