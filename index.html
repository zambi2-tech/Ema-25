How support is detected

Tap EMA 25 or Trendline at the top to choose the support line. The percentages show how far price is above (+) or below (−) that line, and the Min button sets how many bounces a support needs before it's marked. A dip or touch counts when a candle's low comes within about a quarter of a normal candle's range of the line.

EMA 25

Using the last 40 closed candles, the EMA 25 counts as support when it's sloping up, price has dipped to it and closed back above it at least your minimum number of times since it last closed below it, and no candle has closed below it since then.

Trendline

The app finds swing lows (the lowest low among 3 candles on each side), tries every rising line through two of them over the last 100 candles, and throws out any line a candle has closed below. The line with the most touches is used. Every touch counts as a bounce, including the two lows that form the line, so 3 or more is a confirmed trendline. A dash means no rising trendline was found on that timeframe.

Markers

A gold line under a cell means support is holding, and the number is how many bounces it has had. A gold box means price is sitting on the support right now. A red dashed line means price has dropped below a support that was holding; the break is confirmed if the candle closes there.

Tap any token to see its chart with the trendline drawn in, plus the exact trendline and EMA 25 prices.

These are rule-based signals from past candles, not predictions.
