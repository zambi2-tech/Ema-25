How support is detected

On the EMA 25 and Trendline tabs, the percentages show how far price is above (+) or below (−) that line, and the Min button sets how many bounces a support needs before it's marked. A dip or touch counts when a candle's low comes within about a quarter of a normal candle's range of the line.

EMA 25

Using the last 40 closed candles, the EMA 25 counts as support when it's sloping up, price has dipped to it and closed back above it at least your minimum number of times since it last closed below it, and no candle has closed below it since then.

Trendline

The app finds swing lows (the lowest low among 3 candles on each side), tries every rising line through two of them over the last 100 candles, and throws out any line a candle has closed below. The line with the most touches is used. Every touch counts as a bounce, including the two lows that form the line, so 3 or more is a confirmed trendline. A dash means no rising trendline was found on that timeframe.

Markers

A gold line under a cell means support is holding, and the number is how many bounces it has had. A gold box means price is sitting on the support right now. A red dashed line means price has dropped below a support that was holding; the break is confirmed if the candle closes there.

Reward to risk (R:R tab)

For each timeframe, the app takes the nearest support below the price that meets your Min settings from the EMA 25 and Trendline tabs. If the EMA 25 and the trendline are within half a normal candle's range of each other, they count as one support zone ("Both") and the history of both lines is used.

Entry is the current price. The stop sits half a normal candle's range below the support (below the lower line for a zone). The target is the support plus the median size of its past bounces, where each bounce is the highest price reached within 20 candles of an earlier test, or before the next test. R:R is (target − entry) ÷ (entry − stop). A dash means there's no support below price; 0 means price has already risen past the typical bounce.

Setups where price is testing the support now score highest, because the stop is close. The chart for each token shows the stop and target lines. Past bounces don't guarantee future ones.

Pump tab

Each cell shows how much the price has moved over the last 3 candles of that timeframe: the candle forming now and the two before it. On 15m that is roughly the last 45 minutes, and on 1H roughly the last 3 hours. "vol ×3.2" means the last closed candle traded 3.2 times its usual volume (the median of the 20 candles before it). A gold box marks a rise backed by at least twice the usual volume. Tokens are ranked on the timeframe you sort by, from the fastest rise down, and the filters use that timeframe too.

ADX tab

ADX (14) measures how strong a trend is: below 20 is weak, above 25 is a strong trend and above 40 is very strong. Green means an uptrend (+DI above −DI) and red means a downtrend. "Rising" means ADX went up on the last closed candle, so the trend is gaining strength. Uptrend shows only uptrends, and Rising ADX shows only uptrends whose ADX is rising, marked with a gold box. ADX uses closed candles, and the filters use the timeframe you sort by.

Scalp tab

Runs the scalping routine for you on the most traded tokens (Top 50 by volume unless you pick otherwise) and ranks them from the best opportunity down. Each token gets four checks:

Pump: on 15m, price rose over the last 3 candles, by no more than 8% (so you're not chasing), with at least twice the usual volume. ADX: on 15m, an uptrend with ADX of 25 or more and rising. MACD: above its signal line on 1H, 15m and 3m, with a new crossover or a rising histogram on 3m. R:R: a setup with reward to risk of 2 or more on 15m or 3m.

A ✓ marks each check that passes, and tokens that pass all four get a gold edge. Tap a token to see its 15m chart with the entry, stop and target. The Scalp tab always loads 1H, 15m and 3m, even if they're switched off above.

MACD tab

MACD (12, 26, 9) on each timeframe, using the live price for the candle forming now. Each cell shows the PPO, which is the MACD as a percentage of the price, so tokens with very different prices can be compared. Green means MACD is above its signal line and red means below. "hist ↑" means the histogram is rising, and a gold box marks a new bullish crossover near the zero line.

Choose the timeframe with the button at the end of the filter row, then switch on any mix of filters. Tokens must pass all of the ones you switch on. Above signal: MACD is above its signal line. Above 0: MACD is above the zero line. Hist rising: the histogram is higher than on the last closed candle. New cross: MACD crossed above its signal line on the current candle or one of the two before it, close to the zero line (within 20% of the biggest MACD swing over the last 50 candles). Above signal on: tap one or more timeframes on the second row, and only tokens whose MACD is above its signal line on every timeframe you tapped are listed. Tap a timeframe again to remove it.
